RACE MODE

Race to the finish line against local CPU opponents — offline, no online multiplayer. Pick how many rivals line up against you.

Bump a rival and you'll just lose a little speed — but crash into regular traffic and it's still game over, so keep dodging.

HOW TO PLAY

⌨ KEYBOARD

  • ◀▶ or AD — Steer
  • ▲ or W — Accelerate
  • ▼ or S — Brake / Reverse
  • SPACE — Handbrake / Drift
  • N — Nitro Boost
  • P / ESC — Pause

📱 TOUCH

  • Left / Right pads — Steer
  • GAS button — Accelerate
  • BRAKE button — Brake / Reverse
  • DRIFT button — Handbrake
  • NOS button — Nitro Boost

Weave through traffic, grab NOS pickups, and don't crash — every meter counts toward your score. Hit each level's distance goal to level up: the world's theme, the time of day, and your car's paint job all change as you climb through 50 levels.
